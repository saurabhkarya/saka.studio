How is this different from hiring a Zapier freelancer?
A freelancer builds the one workflow you ask for. We map how work moves through your whole firm first, then design and build the system around it, so the automations fit together and someone owns them after launch.
What if our tools can’t be automated?
Most modern CRM, billing and project tools have an API or a Make or Zapier connector. Where one does not, we use email parsing, scheduled exports or a light AI step, and we tell you during the audit if something is not worth automating.
How long does implementation take?
The audit and blueprint take about two weeks. Most firms have their first automations live within 30 days, and the full rollout follows the timeline in your roadmap.
What’s the guarantee?
If the automations in your blueprint do not save your team at least 20 hours a week within 30 days of going live, we refund the blueprint fee.
Do you provide ongoing support?
Yes. Every project ends with a maintenance playbook and a handover session, and you can keep us on a monthly support plan for monitoring, fixes and new automations.
